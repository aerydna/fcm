# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **At work** | Cards a player revealed in Phase 1 to use this turn; they act and are placed in the company structure. |
| **On the beach** | Owned cards not used this turn. Cannot act, can be trained, still cost salary unless fired. |
| **Bank** | Shared money pool. Starts at $50 × players ($75 in the intro game). Breaking it twice ends the game. |
| **Bank reserve card** | Chosen at setup; sets how much money is added when the bank first breaks and the new CEO slot count. Shows a money value + 2/3/4 slots. |
| **Block** | An area enclosed by roads (and possibly the board edge); the area a **mailbox** campaign reaches. |
| **Busy chip** | Numbered chip placed on a marketeer running a campaign; equals the remaining campaign duration. The marketeer takes no slot while busy. |
| **Chain** | A player's company: its restaurants plus its shared pool of food/drinks. |
| **COMING SOON / WELCOME** | The two sides of a restaurant. New restaurants from a local manager start COMING SOON and flip to WELCOME at end of turn. |
| **Company structure / pyramid** | The org chart: CEO on top (3 slots), managers under him (2–10 slots each), normal employees under managers. |
| **Demand token** | A wooden good placed on a house by a marketing campaign, indicating what the house wants to buy. Max 3 per house (5 with a garden). |
| **Drink source** | A drink symbol printed on a map square; buyers collect drinks from adjacent road squares. |
| **Drive-in** | While a local/regional manager is active, that chain's restaurants have entrances in each corner. |
| **Entry position** | A recruitable card (recruit icon top-left); the only cards a recruiter can hire. |
| **Eternal campaign** | A campaign that never ends (from "First billboard placed"); marketeer is gone for the game. |
| **Garden** | Extends a house into a 2×3 rectangle. Doubles the **unit price** portion of income; raises the demand cap to 5. |
| **Milestone** | A one-time achievement granting a permanent effect; lockable for others after the turn it's first claimed. |
| **Open slot** | A CEO/manager slot not filled by a card; determines order-of-play priority. |
| **Range** | Number of tile-border crossings, following roads (or ignoring roads for a zeppelin). Used by buyers, marketeers, and distance calculations. |
| **Reserve card** | See *Bank reserve card*. |
| **Salary** | $5 per card with a salary icon, paid in Phase 5. Entry cards and the CEO have none. |
| **Top position** | A card marked so a player may own only **one** copy (e.g. HR Director, Guru, Zeppelin Pilot, Brand Director, CFO). |
| **Turn order track** | Holds one spot per player; position chosen in Phase 2 by open-slot count. |
| **Unit price** | Base $10 per good, modified by pricing/discount/luxury managers and "First to lower prices". Determines where houses eat. |
