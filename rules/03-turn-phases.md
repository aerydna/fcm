# Turn structure — the 7 phases

A turn is played phase by phase. Some phases are simultaneous; others use turn order.

At any time **except Phase 1**, any player may ask to see another player's cards
(both **at work** and **on the beach**).

---

## Phase 1 — Restructuring (simultaneous)

Each player simultaneously chooses which of their employees to use this turn and
places them **face down**. All other owned cards go face down in a separate pile.
After everyone has chosen, players reveal simultaneously.

- Revealed cards = **"at work"** this turn.
- The rest = **"on the beach"**: cannot act this turn, but can be **trained**, and
  still require a **salary** unless fired.

First turn: only the **CEO** is played (the CEO is **always at work**).

**Building the company pyramid** (all turns after the first): after revealing,
the at-work cards are placed into a company structure — a pyramid:

- Top = the **CEO** (reports to nobody).
- The CEO has up to **3 slots** (shown as rectangles). Cards under the CEO may be
  normal staff **or managers** (black cards).
- Each **manager** has **2–10 slots** (printed number). Cards under a manager may
  only be **normal employees, not other managers**.

If a player placed more cards than fit, all cards except the CEO go to the beach
and the player plays the turn with only the CEO.

After bank reserve cards are opened (when the bank breaks), CEOs may gain/lose a
slot; use a reserve card to indicate the new CEO slot count.

---

## Phase 2 — Order of business (choose turn order)

Players choose their position on the turn-order track. The player with the **most
open slots** chooses first, then the next, etc.

- **Open slot** = a CEO/manager slot not filled by an employee card.
- **"First airplane campaign"** milestone: counts **+2** extra open slots here.
- Tie-break: the player who was **ahead in turn order last turn** chooses first.

There are only as many spots as there are players (with 3 players you cannot pick
the 4th position).

---

## Phase 3 — Working 9:00–5:00 (turn order)

In turn order, each player performs **one action per at-work card**. A player
completes **all** their actions before the next player begins.

Some actions are **mandatory**: pricing manager, discount manager, luxury manager,
CFO, recruiting manager, HR director, and waitress. All others are optional.

Actions are always resolved in this fixed order (card position in the pyramid does
**not** matter):

1. **Recruit**
2. **Train**
3. **Initiate marketing campaigns**
4. **Get food & drinks**
5. **Place new houses and gardens**
6. **Place or move restaurants**

### 3.1 Recruit

Every company gets **one free** recruitment action from the CEO. Additional
recruitment actions come from recruiting cards. For each recruitment action, hire
**one** employee at an **entry position** (recruit icon in the top-left). Add hired
cards to your **beach** pile.

- If a deck is empty, you cannot hire that type until copies return — **unless**
  you immediately **train** it higher this turn (then you don't take the card).
- Recruitment actions: recruiting girl (×1), recruiting manager (×2), HR director
  (×4). **Unused** recruiting-manager/HR-director actions become a **salary
  discount** in Payday (see below).

### 3.2 Train

For each **training action** in your structure you may train **one person one
step**. You may only train cards **on the beach** (including just-hired ones).
Cards currently **at work** and **busy marketeers** cannot be trained.

- Marketeers, kitchen staff, and buyers each have their **own upgrade track**. All
  other non-entry employees are trained directly from managers (black cards).
- Return the card being trained to stock and replace it with the target card.
- Normally you may not train the same employee twice in a turn. Exceptions:
  - **Coach**: may train the same card **two steps**.
  - **Guru**: may train the same card **up to three steps**.
  - **"First to pay $20 in salaries"** milestone: may use multiple trainers/
    coaches/gurus on the **same** card.
- A coach/guru may instead split attention over multiple employees.
- You cannot train to a position if the **final** card isn't in stock. But when
  training multiple steps (or hire-then-train), **intermediate** cards need not be
  available.
- You may own only **one** copy of each card marked as a **top position**.

### 3.3 Initiate marketing campaigns

See [06-marketing.md](06-marketing.md).

### 3.4 Get food & drinks

If you have kitchen staff and/or buyers, collect goods per the card. Collected
goods sit in front of you and are shared by all your restaurants.

- **Kitchen trainee**: 1 burger **or** 1 pizza.
- **Errand boy**: 1 drink of your choice (soft drink, beer, or lemonade).
- **Burger cook/chef**: burgers = number on card (3 / 8).
- **Pizza cook/chef**: pizzas = number on card (3 / 8).
- **Cart operator**: from a restaurant entrance, trace a route of **range 2** over
  roads (up to 3 tiles incl. start). Take **2 drinks per drink symbol** you pass on
  either side of the road (must traverse the road orthogonally adjacent to the
  symbol; no U-turns; need not return).
- **Truck driver**: same as cart operator but **range 3**, **3 drinks** per symbol.
- **Zeppelin pilot**: from a restaurant entrance, trace **range 4 ignoring roads**
  (up to 5 tiles, no tile twice). Collect **2 drinks per drink symbol** on flown
  tiles (multiple symbols on a tile all count).
- **"First errand boy played"** milestone: **+1 drink per source**; errand boys
  bring **2** identical drinks. **"First cart operator played"** milestone: cart
  operators/truck drivers/zeppelin pilots get **+1 range**.

### 3.5 Place new houses and gardens (new business developer)

- **New house**: place in any empty area connected to at least one road. No range
  limit. Houses placed by a developer **come with a garden** automatically and
  cannot get another.
- **New garden**: must connect to a **printed** house over 2 squares so the house+
  garden form a **2×3 rectangle**. Only on empty squares; each house gets at most
  one garden. Orient it to show which house it belongs to.

### 3.6 Place or move restaurants (local/regional manager)

A restaurant must be placed fully on empty squares, entrance adjacent to a road.
Unlike setup, the entrance **may** be on the same tile as another restaurant's
entrance.

- **Local manager**: place a **new** restaurant within **range 3 tiles by road**
  from an existing restaurant of the chain. Placed **COMING SOON** (opens at end of
  turn). While a local manager is active, that chain's restaurants have a
  **drive-in** (entrances in each corner).
- **Regional manager**: place a restaurant **anywhere** (entrance on a road). May
  also **pick up and move/rotate** an existing restaurant. Restaurants placed/moved
  by a regional manager are **active immediately** (no COMING SOON). While active,
  the chain's restaurants have a **drive-in**.

## Phase 5 — Payday

Each chain may **fire** any number of employees (structure or beach); fired cards
return to stock.

- Busy marketeers normally may **not** be fired.
- Pay **$5** for each card in structure or on the beach that shows a **salary**
  icon. Paid wages return to the bank.
- Busy marketeers with a salary icon **must** still be paid. If a player cannot pay
  a busy marketeer and has already fired all other salaried employees, the
  marketeer **must be fired** (their campaign remains on the board).
- **Recruiting manager / HR director** unused hiring actions each give a **$5
  salary discount**. **All available discounts must be used** (relevant for the
  "First to pay $20 in salaries" milestone). Minimum salary paid is **$0**.

Salary-reducing effects also come from milestones ("First to train someone" = $15
discount) and card abilities.

---

## Phase 6 — Marketing campaigns (resolve)

All campaigns fire in order of their **printed number** (lowest first). See
[06-marketing.md](06-marketing.md) for how each campaign type places demand tokens.

- Each house holds at most **3** demand tokens (**5** with a garden). Excess is
  wasted.
- After a campaign fires, remove **one** advertising token from it (unless
  eternal). If it has no tokens left, return it to stock and free the marketeer.
  Otherwise it stays and the marketeer stays busy.

---

## Phase 7 — Clean up

- Players **without** the "First to throw away food/drink" milestone must discard
  any remaining food/drinks. (With that milestone: store up to 10 in the freezer.)
- All cards from structure and beach return to hand (others may inspect them now).
- Flip all **COMING SOON** restaurants to **WELCOME**.
- If any milestones were awarded this turn, flip the remaining copies of **those**
  milestone types face down — no longer available.
- Advertising tokens on campaigns and demand tokens on houses **remain** on the board.

The next turn starts at Phase 1.
